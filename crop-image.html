What is Image Cropping?

Simple Explanation

Image cropping is the process of removing unwanted outer areas of an image. It allows you to focus on the subject and improve composition.

Why Cropping Matters

Cropping helps improve visual quality, remove distractions, and fit images into required dimensions for websites or social media.
