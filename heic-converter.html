HEIC to JPG / PNG / WebP Converter Online

The HEIC Converter Tool allows you to convert iPhone photos (HEIC format) into widely supported formats like JPG, PNG, and WebP. This tool works directly in your browser without installing any software.

HEIC is the default image format used by Apple devices such as iPhones and iPads. However, many websites, apps, and Windows devices cannot open HEIC files. That is why converting HEIC images is important.

What is HEIC?

HEIC stands for High Efficiency Image Container. It is a modern image format used by Apple devices to save storage space while maintaining high image quality.

Although HEIC is efficient, it is not widely supported on all devices. That is why users often need to convert HEIC images into JPG or PNG formats.

After conversion, you can also use our Image Compressor to reduce file size or the Image Resizer to adjust dimensions.

How This Tool Works

This tool reads your HEIC image in the browser, converts it into a canvas format, and then exports it as JPG, PNG, or WebP.

Everything happens locally in your browser, which means your images are not uploaded anywhere.

Why Convert HEIC Images?

  • Upload images to websites that don’t support HEIC
  • Share photos on social media easily
  • Open images on Windows or Android devices
  • Convert iPhone photos for editing tools
