What is HEIC?

Simple Explanation

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Format) is used by Apple devices like iPhones to store photos in smaller file sizes without losing quality.

Why It Needs Conversion

Many devices, apps, and websites do not support HEIC files, which is why converting them to JPG or PNG is important.
