Why Use WebP Format?

WebP is designed by Google to reduce image size while keeping high quality. It is now supported by all modern browsers and is one of the best formats for websites, blogs, and online stores.

Using WebP can improve page speed, reduce bandwidth usage, and increase SEO rankings because Google prefers fast-loading websites.
