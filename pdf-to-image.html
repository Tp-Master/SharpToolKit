Why Convert PDF to Image?

Many people need to turn PDF pages into image files for sharing, editing, presentations, websites, or social media. Images are easier to upload to many platforms than PDF files. JPG files are ideal for smaller file sizes, while PNG files are great for sharp graphics and text.
